# Require human asset release declarations

Formal asset-pack release requires a person or authorized organization to
confirm the attribution author, license and source declaration, every warning
acknowledgement reason, and the final attributed preview. Agents may collect
evidence, propose compatible choices, and draft explanations, but they may not
invent identity, license authority, risk acceptance, or visual approval. Draft
generation and preview may continue without those declarations, preserving a
smooth authoring loop while keeping formal publication tied to accountable
human decisions.
